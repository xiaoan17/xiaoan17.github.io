Efficient Large-Scale Traffic Flow Forecasting via Multi-Subgraph Spatio-Temporal Graph Convolutional Networks
0 引用
Journal Journal of Intelligent Transportation Systems 2026
Bocheng An, Chuang Cui, Rui Gan, Xu Qu, Bin Ran
Scholar ↗
展开摘要 ▾
Proposes MuSTGCN, a Multi-Subgraph Spatio-Temporal Graph Convolutional Network for large-scale traffic flow prediction. Divides the graph into subgraphs around pivotal nodes, trains each subgraph separately, and merges outputs. Validates on 8,600-node real-world datasets with significantly reduced training/inference time and GPU memory.
